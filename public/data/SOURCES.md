# Namqula language data: sources and reuse

## AISOD corpus and contributor acknowledgement

Namqula credits the **[AISOD Corpus of Language Data](https://corpus.aisod.tech) and all its contributors** for corpus stewardship, community knowledge and the mission of language, cultural and data preservation. Help enrich the corpus with more words, languages, context and recordings.

This acknowledgement accompanies every bundled vocabulary record. Original authors, translators, source links and licences remain credited below and in each record. Some starter entries were curated directly from the named public sources; this credit does not claim they were all downloaded from the corpus website or transfer their ownership to AISOD.

Prepared 1 November 2026. These are source-backed starter vocabulary packs, not complete dictionaries or certification by native-speaker reviewers. Linguistic approval, example sentences and recordings remain a separate editorial step.

## Oshindonga (96 entries)

Source: AISOD local `Oshindonga_Corpus_Organized/dictionary_records.jsonl`, derived from [abiatarfestus/oshinglish](https://github.com/abiatarfestus/oshinglish).

Licence: MIT; the complete upstream notice is in [LICENSE-oshinglish.txt](LICENSE-oshinglish.txt). The local source SHA-256 is recorded in manifest.json. Each entry retains its original sourceRecordId. The import selects specific record IDs and assigns learning categories; it does not machine-translate or alter the source spellings. The raw export has 6,526 records, including 159 Python built-in records, which are excluded. Other dictionary senses and bound forms are deliberately not imported wholesale.

This is **Oshindonga**, not Oshikwanyama. Oshikwanyama has its own separate course and vocabulary pack; its existing URL continues to open Oshikwanyama. Old unverified built-in vocabulary is removed from the runtime. Existing database rows are not deleted.

## Khoekhoegowab (44 entries)

Source: Wiktionary contributors, [Appendix:Khoekhoe Swadesh list](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Khoekhoe_Swadesh_list), consulted 1 November 2026. Authors and revisions: [page history](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=Appendix:Khoekhoe_Swadesh_list&action=history).

Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: selected standalone forms, selected senses, categories and stable identifiers. Abbreviated noun endings, alternatives with slashes and optional-letter conventions were omitted instead of guessing their expansion. This adapted vocabulary pack is distributed under the same licence. Click letters and vowel marks are preserved.

## Expanding the courses

A dictionary entry is not automatically a good game question. Keep variety, part of speech and sense explicit. Review translations with speakers before describing them as approved. Add provenance and licences to each imported record; reject executable metadata, missing translations and duplicate prompt/answer sets. Do not copy commercial APK dictionaries or assume that a free download permits redistribution.

## Oshikwanyama (111 entries)

Source: Thera Crane, Karl Lindgren-Streicher and Andy Wingo, **Hai ti! — A Beginner’s Guide to Oshikwanyama** (2004), https://wingolog.org/pub/hai-ti/hai-ti.pdf. The source’s copyright page explicitly grants [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

The adapted Oshikwanyama JSON pack is offered under that same CC BY-SA 2.0 licence. Changes: selected complete forms from the vocabulary tables; selected one explicitly listed alternative where appropriate; added categories, identifiers and page references; simplified English glosses while retaining relevant context. The original spellings are preserved. Pronouns, counting forms, greetings and possessive family terms stay in their own contexts. The author list, source, page and licence remain available with each word. No illustrations, proverbs or third-party song lyrics were imported.

Cross-checks: WikiEducator’s versions of [chapter 4](https://wikieducator.org/index.php?title=Oshikwanyama/Oshifima_oshinyenye&oldid=596606) and [chapter 5](https://wikieducator.org/index.php?title=Oshikwanyama/Ovaneumbo_vange&oldid=596607). This is source-backed curation, not a claim that AISOD has obtained fresh native-speaker approval of every entry.

## Chokwe (24 starter entries)

Adapted from Wikipedia contributors, [Tchokwé (langue), revision 224888978](https://fr.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tchokw%C3%A9_(langue)&oldid=224888978), especially the complete noun examples and orthography section citing Boubacar Diarra (1987/1988). Distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: selected singular words and complete infinitives, translated short French glosses into English, assigned learning categories and identifiers. Source tones and vowel length are retained. The pack does not turn ASJP phonetic codes into guessed learner spellings. This is a small source-backed set, not native-speaker certification or a complete Chokwe dictionary.

## Rukwangali (24 starter entries)

Adapted from [Counting animals / Kuvarura yikorama](https://storybooksnamibia.net/stories/kwn/0327/), written by Zanele Buthelezi, Thembani Dladla and Clare Verbeek; **Rukwangali translation by Djaupyu Siteketa**, African Storybook / Storybooks Namibia. The story page grants [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: selected vocabulary in context, retained plural animal forms and explicit noun-agreement contexts for numbers, added learning categories and identifiers. No illustrations or audio copied. The animal forms are not silently changed from plural to singular. The adapted pack remains attributed and reusable under CC BY 3.0. The separate Peace Corps manual, which has a commercial-permission notice at its host, was not imported.

The six packs are independently sourced. “kwangari” and “kwangali” resolve to Rukwangali; Oshikwanyama remains separate from Oshindonga.
